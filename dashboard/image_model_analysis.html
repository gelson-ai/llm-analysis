Same metric, different units. Performance here is every judged check a model passed divided by every check it attempted, pooled flat across its benchmark prompts. Price is the mean cost_usd of those same generations — not the catalogue's per-image, per-megapixel or per-token rate — so performance and price come from identical rows and are directly comparable to each other. Cost comparability caveat: no benchmark row records the resolution or settings it was generated at, so these costs are comparable across models only if the benchmark harness held resolution fixed; this dataset cannot verify that. Design Arena scores are shown separately and never affect the value score, and no Artificial Analysis data exists for image models at all.
